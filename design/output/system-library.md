# System library

Reader: whoever adds an example system or changes how systems load.

**A system is a complete example: its input signals, its plant (diagram, with any Controller blocks and their variants) and its views. It loads whole or by part, so the input of one system can drive the plant of another.** (The person, 2026-10-24: "call them system library".)

- **Tags** (the person, 2026-10-24): each system shows coloured tags in the System library: linear or nonlinear, dead time, its controller and observer types, a disturbance, frequency views, HIL, auto-tune. They are derived from the system itself (`systemTags` in `src/core/systems.js`); an entry may add its own. *Variable structure* is a class of controller of its own (the person, [input 38](../input/2026-10-25-38-variable-structure-tag.md)): the laws that switch their structure or gain with the state carry it (relay, sliding mode, super-twisting, Hu–Lin's variable gain; interpretation: the class replaces the *Sliding mode* and *Relay* tags and, on Hu–Lin, *State feedback*); it takes Sliding mode's red. A system with an External plant is tagged **Black box** (kind `plant`, yellow): its physics is outside the page and the page does not know it (input 20); an entry may still name its own plant tags. Values that differ look different at a glance (the person): the style says what a tag is about (plant: a filled chip; controller: a coloured bar; observer: an outline; feature: a dot; *No controller*: an empty dashed chip) and the tone which value it is, the same on every card. Linear and Nonlinear take the cool and warm poles (blue, red). No tag shares its tone with a tag beside it on a card, and no two values of one kind share one in the filter row: *Disturbance* is slate (it was Nonlinear's red on three cards, then violet, which is the selection's colour), so *XY* is yellow, and *LQR*, beside Disturbance on both pendulums, is orange (State feedback took slate while it was on no card; back on Gear backlash's, it is blue, the one tone no controller tag has, and Deadbeat keeps slate); *Dead time* is magenta (it was Auto-tune's green on the oven). *Disturbance* shows only where d plays something (a Source d whose signal has components; *Following a sine* keeps an empty d to fill). Tones come from an eight-slot palette validated for colour-vision deficiency on both themes' panels; the label is always shown, and a key under the popover's heading names the five styles. **Filtering** (the person): under the key, every tag in the library is a chip; a click on one (there or on a card) toggles it as a filter, and the list keeps the systems that carry all chosen tags (interpretation: several tags narrow), with a count and Clear (`libraryTags`, `filterByTags` in `systems.js`).

## What each design computes in the page

The library holds design **inputs** only: physics, hand gains, and the weights, levels and limits of designed variants. Every gain, table and start value a designed variant runs on is computed in the page from the plant as it is wired, every time the diagram compiles (cached in memory), so changing a mass or a radius changes the gains with it ([designs.md](designs.md); the person, [input 09](../input/2026-10-24-09-analysis-autotune-triple-swingup.md)). The Controller tab shows what was computed, in the **Computed** box under the parameters; **Copy as numbers** turns it into a hand variant.

- **DC motor**: hand gains, no design. P with Kp = 30 V/rad against 12 V limits rings; PD (Kp 20, Kd 1.5) is damped near ζ = 0.65; the PID adds Ki 10 and a 0.1 s prefilter. Its Motor is built from Transfer function blocks (above), so the design layer sees the current i and the speed as its states.
- **Encoder example**: a hand PI.
- **Gear backlash**: hand gains for about 30 rad/s on b′ = K·G/(G²·Jm + JL) = 55.6 rad/s² per A (Kp = ω²/b′ ≈ 16 A/rad, Kd = 1.4·ω/b′ ≈ 0.76, Ki = 6·Kp ≈ 100, Tf 3 ms, ±5 A); the Dual loop is state feedback holding the PD's gains, i* = 16·(r − θ_L) − 0.76·ωm/G.
- **DC motor, deadbeat** (`deadbeat` and `lqr`; inputs: Update every 10 or 1, limits ±12 V or none; LQR Q = [100, 1, 0], R = 1): both on the motor linearised at the origin with angle, speed and current measured. Deadbeat discretises it at k·Ts (100 ms: K = 8.00, 0.444, 0.110 on angle, speed, current, kr = 8.00; 10 ms: K = 655, 11.5, 1.61); LQR at 10 ms gives K = 9.10, 0.744, 0.179.
- **DC motor, speed from an encoder** (`luenbergerPlaced` and `kalman`, Observer variants; inputs: w = 400 1/s; Q = 1, R empty): both take the model the Observer sees, V → encoder, three states (the armature's current, the rotor's speed, the angle), linearised at the origin. The Luenberger places the error's three poles at s = −400 1/s by Ackermann's formula (L = 601, 216, 0.665); the Kalman filter's R is the encoder's σ² + q²/12, its L = 5.12, 8.08, 0.123. The finite difference holds only its rate filter Tf (0 here), a choice, not a number from the plant. The PI is a hand PI.
- **Following a sine** (`pidFF` for *PID + feedforward*; inputs: the PID's Kp 30, Ki 80, Kd 1, Tf 0.02 s, the velocity term, limits ±12 V): the feedforward is the motor's inverse from V to the angle, (R·b + Kt·Ke)/Kt · s + (R·J + L·b)/Kt · s² + …, plus what the PID's derivative on the angle takes away: Kv = 0.7 + Kd = 1.7 V·s/rad, the number that was once set by hand (it was found by raising Kv until the figure lay flat, and is exactly what the plant gives), and the same run bit for bit. Its Feedforward choice "and acceleration" adds Ka = (R·J + L·b)/Kt − Kd·Tf = 0.022 V·s²/rad: σ past 1.5 s falls from 0.0004 to 0.0001 rad. The other variants (P, PD, PID) are hand gains.
- **Conical tank** (`scheduledPI`; inputs: levels `at`, closed-loop time constant Tcl = 0.25 s, limits 0…3 m³/s): at each level the page trims the tank (the pump flow that holds it), linearises it and fits a first-order lag K/(τs + 1); pole cancellation gives Kp = τ/(K·Tcl), Ki = 1/(K·Tcl). For this tank that is Kp = A(h)/Tcl and Ki = a√(2g)/(2√h·Tcl): Kp 0.113 … 2.87 and Ki 2.80 … 0.909 over 0.1 … 0.95 m. The top level is 0.95 m, not the brim: the tank overflows at its height H = 1 m (the physics, main), so at 1 m the level's rate has a slope below and none above, a kink the design refuses ("pole cancellation needs slopes, and the plant has a kink at h = 1 m"; decisions.md, v2.7.2). The fixed PIs are the same design at one level (0.8 m: Kp 2.112, Ki 0.990; 0.15 m: Kp 0.173, Ki 2.287). u0, the flow that holds the start level 0.15 m, is a√(2g·0.15) = 0.1716, so every PI starts in exact balance. The pipe's 0.1 s dead time is reported and left out of the design; it is what makes the PI tuned for full ring when the level is low.
- **Pendulum** (`swingUpLqr` and `lqr`; inputs: Q = [10, 50, 1, 1] for x, θ, v, ω, R = 0.1, limits ±25 N): discrete LQR (at the block's Ts, 10 ms) on the cart and pole linearised at the upright rest gives K ≈ [−9.18, −63.2, −10.3, −13.3] and kr = K[0], so x follows r. The swing-up's energy pump keeps its hand gains (kE 3, kx 8, kv 5, catch below 0.4 rad), found by simulation: up in about 2 s, cart within ±1.2 m; the pole length and g it measures energy with (0.5 m, 9.81) are computed from the cart and pole too, so a longer pole is pumped to its own top.
- **Triple pendulum** (`trajectory` and `lqr`; Q = [10, 100, 100, 100, 1, 1, 1, 1], R = 0.1, limits ±40 N; the plan: T = 2.4 s, 12 segments, 75 % of the limit): when the system loads the page plans the swing-up from the cart and rods as they are wired (`planner.js`, about 3 s in Node, 5 to 7 s in a browser, during which the display draws at 20 frames a second): a force held per 10 ms sample that takes the chain from hanging to rest upright with the cart back at 0, peak about 15 N of the 30 N it may use. Along it, time-varying LQR (the Riccati recursion backwards from the upright LQR) corrects small errors; after it, the upright LQR (the same gains as "LQR only") holds the rods and takes the push. A check on the nonlinear plant confirms the rods are up 1 s after the plan before it is used; a plan that fails still runs, with a warning, so the attempt can be seen. Nothing of the plan is stored: changing a mass plans again.
- **Oven**: heater (lag 0.1 s), chamber (1 °C per %, lag 0.5 s), 0.1 s dead time to the sensor, thermocouple (lag 0.05 s); heater 0–100 %, Ts 20 ms. Its PID (Kp 6, Ki 12, Kd 0.1) is hand-picked to be poor (42 % overshoot); the Auto-tune tab computes better gains from the plant, nothing is stored ([autotune.md](autotune.md)).
- **Sliding-mode motor**: λ = 10 1/s (the error's time constant 0.1 s; sliding holds for |e| ≤ 2.4 rad even with J×3), K = 12 V (the limit), φ = 2 rad/s (the layer's speed loop β·K/φ = 150 1/s near the armature's corner 100 1/s, ζ ≈ 0.41; thinner layers ring), super-twisting k1 = 4 (a hand gain) and k2 = 44, which is Levant's 1.1·L/β with this motor's β = Kt/(J·R) = 25 and a guessed L = 1000 rad/s³: derived from the plant, so a designed super-twisting would compute it; how it should take β is open (roadmap; [controller-proposal.md](controller-proposal.md)).
- **HIL**: no gains from the plant's physics (it is a black box, input 20): the P (Kp 2, limited to the described ±12 V) is a first guess that moves the example plant without reaching its hard ranges; PD and PID variants tuned by hand against the script's physics were removed. `test/hil-agent.test.mjs` and `test/hil-blackbox.test.mjs` check that the script describes exactly the ports the system's External plant block has, and that the block, its Sinks and channels follow a plant with other ports. It opens with the setup area at 36 %, since Live adds a row to Run Control. A variant from the plant has nothing to design on there: the page has no model of a plant outside it.
- Each design is checked against the closed-form values and the gains the library used to hold in `test/design.test.mjs`; the behaviour of every system in `test/core.test.mjs` (the oven in `test/autotune.test.mjs`, the sliding-mode motor in `test/smc.test.mjs`, the observer example in `test/observer-example.test.mjs`). A guard test refuses stored gain tables, start outputs and pipe start flows in the library; it allows the hand types `pid`, `off`, `slidingMode`, `superTwisting` and `difference` beside the designed ones, and Gear backlash's *Dual loop* (state feedback holding a PD's hand gains). The deadbeat example in `test/deadbeat.test.mjs`.

To add a system: add it to `plants.js` (name, summary (the bottom line: the model, its features, what to try, what you learn; no numbers), notes (the full description), `signals`, `scope`, `ui`, `diagram`, `views`, and `sim: { sampleTime }` when it needs its own record step); it must move visibly with its own input within 5 s, and a test checks its behaviour. A scope channel may be assigned but off, or carry a Measure line: `channels('e', 'speed', { source: 'V', on: false, measure: true })`. Leave the scales to Auto Scale, which runs on load.
